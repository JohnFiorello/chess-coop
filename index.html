Current playtest rules

Human allies block one another but cannot capture one another. Pawns may move two squares on their first move and promote to queens at the far edge. If a human king is checkmated, that entire army is removed. The CPU king cannot be captured; if it is pinned early, the team must still destroy the remaining CPU army. When only the CPU king remains, checkmate wins.
